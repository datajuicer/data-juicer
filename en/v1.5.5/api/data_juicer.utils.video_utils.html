extract_frames_uniformly_by_seconds(frame_num: int, duration: float) → List[ndarray][source]¶

Extract frames uniformly from each segment of given duration.

The video is split into segments of the specified duration, and frame_num frames are extracted uniformly from each segment. Only full-length segments are processed (matching AV behavior); a trailing partial segment shorter than duration is skipped.

Parameters:

  • frame_num – Number of frames per segment.

  • duration – Duration of each segment in seconds.

Returns:

A list of numpy arrays (RGB frames).
